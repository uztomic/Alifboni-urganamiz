## Biz to'playdigan ma'lumotlar

Ilova ro'yxatdan o'tishni yoki shaxsiy ma'lumot kiritishni talab qilmaydi. Bolaning ismi, yoshi, telefon raqami yoki boshqa shaxsiy ma'lumotlari so'ralmaydi va serverga yuborilmaydi.

Ilova quyidagi ma'lumotlarni faqat **qurilmaning o'zida** (mahalliy, Hive ma'lumotlar bazasida) saqlaydi:
- O'rganish jarayoni (qaysi harflar/so'zlar o'rganilgani, yulduzchalar, ketma-ket kunlar)
- Tanlangan til va sozlamalar
- Ota-ona bo'limi uchun PIN-kod (xeshlangan holda saqlanadi)

Bu ma'lumotlar ilovani o'chirib tashlaganingizda qurilmadan butunlay o'chib ketadi va hech qachon uchinchi tomonlarga yuborilmaydi.

## Reklamalar (Google AdMob)

Ilova Google AdMob orqali reklamalarni ko'rsatadi (banner, o'tish (interstitial), mukofotli (rewarded) va native reklamalar). AdMob reklamalarni ko'rsatish uchun qurilma identifikatorlari va reklama bilan o'zaro aloqa haqidagi ma'lumotlarni to'plashi mumkin.

Ilova AdMob’da **"bolalarga mo'ljallangan" (child-directed treatment)** sozlamasi yoqilgan holda ishlaydi:
- `tagForChildDirectedTreatment: true`
- `tagForUnderAgeOfConsent: true`
- `maxAdContentRating: G`

Bu sozlamalar shaxsiylashtirilmagan (contextual) reklamalarni ta'minlashga va bolalarga mos bo'lmagan reklama kontentini cheklashga yordam beradi. AdMobning o'z maxfiylik siyosati bilan tanishish uchun: https://policies.google.com/privacy

## Internet aloqasi

Ilova ba'zi funksiyalar (masalan, tabiat bo'limidagi rasmlar, reklamalar) uchun internetga ulanishni talab qiladi. Internet aloqasi yo'q bo'lganda, tegishli bo'limlar ishlamaydi va foydalanuvchiga bu haqda ko'rsatiladi.

# Privacy Policy — Alifboni o'rganish (English)

*Last updated: 2026-07-19*

"Alifboni o'rganish" is a children's app for learning the alphabet, vocabulary, and nature facts in multiple languages.

**Data we collect:** none directly. The app requires no sign-up and no personal information (name, age, phone number, etc.) is ever requested or transmitted. Learning progress, star ratings, streaks, language preference, and a hashed parent-zone PIN are stored **locally on the device only** (via Hive) and are deleted when the app is uninstalled.

**Advertising:** the app shows ads via Google AdMob (banner, interstitial, rewarded, and native ads). AdMob may collect device identifiers and ad-interaction data to serve ads. The app is configured with child-directed treatment (`tagForChildDirectedTreatment`, `tagForUnderAgeOfConsent`, `maxAdContentRating: G`) to request non-personalized, contextual ads appropriate for children. See Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

**Internet access:** some features (nature-category photos, ads) require an internet connection; without one, those sections are unavailable and the app tells the user so.
